Privacy

Last updated 2026-08-25

Distanser collects no data, from you or about you. It has no network access, no analytics, and no crash reporting. Nothing you do in Distanser is sent anywhere, by Distanser or by us.

Distanser reads the pointer's position, held mouse buttons, and modifier keys to draw its rulers, crosshair and measurements — this happens locally and is never stored or transmitted. Your ruler positions, zero marks, guides and preferences are saved with UserDefaults on your own Mac and never leave it.

Distanser asks for no permissions — no accessibility access, no screen recording, no camera, microphone or location.

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
